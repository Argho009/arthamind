## 📌 Post 2 (Build Progress): The Technical Breakthrough
**Goal:** Showcase engineering depth, architectural diagrams, and a key lesson learned.

```markdown
Quick update from the Agentic AI Hackathon floor 🛠️

Here is our #1 architectural rule for building financial AI:
👉 The LLM should NEVER do arithmetic.

If a user asks: "What happens if I cut dining out by 30% or if my rent increases by ₹3,500?", letting a generative LLM calculate compound growth or goal dates leads to dangerous hallucinations and broken trust.

Instead, we decoupled the intelligence layer into two strict components:
1. NLU Intent Parser (LLM): Extracts structured scenario parameters into clean JSON.
2. Deterministic Simulation Engine: Unit-tested mathematical calculations that project goal dates and runway with 100% precision.

Coupled with a tested SEBI-compliant guardrail layer to ensure responsible financial education without unlicensed stock tips.

Code is compiling and deployed to Cloudflare D1. Final demo coming up next! 🚀

#SoftwareEngineering #FintechArchitecture #SEBI #DeterministicAI #ProductSpace #BuildInPublic
```
